# V89 — original territory illustrations
Eleven generated, standalone color illustrations, cropped from 2K sheets without enlarging the source. Nine scenes have native 911–912 × 506 pixels; Congo and Angola have 1370 × 1530. All are optimized local WebP files, shared by the territory cards and removable photographs inside the drawers. The old nine low-resolution sprite crops and the incorrect Angola composition are not used by the territory inventory. No external image URLs or installation are required at runtime. The five-step parchment and drawer alignment were refined after reviewing the V88 browser screenshots. The bank has an explicit neutral empty state once all 11 territories are assigned, independent of final correctness. Teacher-only release remains unchanged.
